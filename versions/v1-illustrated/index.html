01
Chapter 01 — The Captain

It starts on
the board.

Er. Raja Singh · Team Lead

Before a single wire gets cut, Raja maps the whole episode: the idea, the build plan, the shoot, the budget and the deadline. When the plan breaks (it always does), he's the one redrawing it at midnight.

“If it doesn't scare us a little, it's not a Builders.mu video.”— Raja, every Monday
